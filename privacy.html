🛡️ Privacy Policy for CashKi

Thank you for using CashKi. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

- Cash denomination records (e.g., notes count, total amounts)
- Basic device information (used only for app diagnostics)
- No sensitive personal data is collected or stored

2. How We Use Your Information

The information is used only for calculating and saving your cash records locally on your device. We do not access, view, or transmit this data to any server.

3. Data Storage and Security

All your data is securely stored locally using Hive database. No data is uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

4. Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used solely for identifying you within the app. It does not give us access to your personal data, emails, or contacts. No user data is uploaded to Firebase.

5. Use of Advertisements

CashKi displays ads to support development. We use Google AdMob to show:

  • Banner Ads
  • Interstitial Ads
  • Full-Screen/Rewarded Ads
These ads may collect limited, non-personal data (like ad performance) as per AdMob’s policy. For more details, read Google Ad Policy.

6. Data Retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it manually or uninstall the app. You can clear your records using the in-app Clear Data option.

7. Your Choices

You may use the app without signing in if you wish. You are also in control of your stored data and may delete it anytime.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Any updates to this policy will be reflected within the app. Continued use of the app implies agreement with the latest version.
